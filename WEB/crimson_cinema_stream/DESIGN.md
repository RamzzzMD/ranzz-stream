## Brand & Style

The design system powers an immersive, high-intensity entertainment streaming interface focused on Asian dramas, blockbuster cinema, and anime. The aesthetic is cinematic, premium, and focused—utilizing an ultra-deep charcoal and obsidian foundation illuminated by vivid crimson sparks and directional lighting to elevate rich poster art and video previews.

### Personality & Emotional Response
- **Cinematic & Immersive:** Dark stage lighting pulls visual focus directly to vibrant thumbnails, keyart, and dynamic playback trailers.
- **Electric & Passionate:** Controlled bursts of vivid crimson red (`#FF2A3A`) inject energy, acting as vital navigation beacons, rating cues, and active indicators.
- **Crisp & Fluid:** Uncluttered layouts, generous hit targets, smooth transitions, and tactile dark card elevated surfaces ensure effortless browsing across sprawling catalogs.

### Design Movement
Modern Cinematic Dark Mode with subtle ambient glow accents. Flat dark tonal layering combines with fine translucent borders, micro-glows behind primary calls-to-action, and rounded container geometry.

## Colors

The palette is tuned specifically for low-light digital entertainment consumption, minimizing eye strain while maximizing media vibrancy and contrast.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary (`#FF2A3A`):** The primary brand neon-crimson. Used for main playback action buttons ("Mulai Nonton", "Lanjutkan"), active tab highlights, progress indicators, and bright glow effects.
- **Secondary (`#E50914`):** Deep cinematic scarlet. Used for hover states on primary components, active navigation pills, and category tag fills.
- **Tertiary (`#FFAA00`):** Vivid amber star-rating color. Reserved strictly for critique scores, review aggregators, and golden highlights.
- **Surface & Background Hierarchy:**
  - `Canvas / Absolute Base`: `#0B0B0E` (Deepest abyss black for full viewport backdrops).
  - `Surface Low (Cards, Sidebars)`: `#141419` (Elevated card containers, navigation rails, and bottom sheets).
  - `Surface Mid (Input, Dropdowns, Hovered Cards)`: `#1C1C24` (Search bars, tertiary buttons, and popover menus).
  - `Surface High / Active`: `#262632` (Pill borders, interactive toggle tracks).
- **Text & Content Levels:**
  - `Text High Contrast`: `#FFFFFF` (Primary titles, movie names, active tabs).
  - `Text Medium Contrast`: `#E2E8F0` (Body descriptions, episode summaries, actor names).
  - `Text Low Contrast / Muted`: `#94A3B8` (Metadata, durations, release dates, secondary icons).
  - `Border / Hairline Outline`: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` (Subtle separation for cards, dividers, and input outlines).

## Typography

The design system exclusively adopts **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all levels to balance high-tech geometric modernity with humanistic warmth and clean legibility against dark backdrops.

### Hierarchy Guidelines
- **Hero & Title Display (`display-hero` / `headline-xl`):** Tight letter tracking (-0.02em to -0.03em) with bold weights to produce commanding headlines on featured banner carousels.
- **Section Headers (`headline-lg` / `headline-md`):** Crisp and clean dividers for movie rows (e.g., "Populer Hari Ini", "Daftar Tontonan", "Katalog").
- **Body & Synopsis (`body-lg` / `body-md`):** Generous line height (1.5–1.6) using slate tones (`#E2E8F0` / `#94A3B8`) to provide relaxed reading of plot synopses and metadata.
- **Metadata & Badges (`label-sm` / `label-md`):** Capitalized tracking (+0.03em) and bold styling for resolution tags (e.g., "HD", "4K"), ratings ("⭐ 9.5"), and category badges.

## Layout & Spacing

A fluid grid architecture engineered for high-density visual media galleries, horizontal scrolling media carousels, and edge-to-edge cinematic banner headers.

### Grid System
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout with 24px gutters and 48px outer canvas margin. Max content container width: 1440px.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 32px canvas margin.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 16px gutters and 16px outer margins. Content feeds collapse to single or dual-column grids; hero sliders adopt full-bleed bleeding layouts with 16px horizontal internal padding.

### Media Card Grids
- **Landscape Video Cards (Episodes/History):** 16:9 aspect ratio, fixed vertical list or 3-column desktop layout.
- **Portrait Keyart (Dramas, Movies, Anime):** 2:3 vertical aspect ratio, spanning 2 columns on desktop (6 items per row) and 2 columns on mobile (2 items per row).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is conveyed through luminous tonal gradation, semi-translucent backplates, and targeted red ambient glows rather than standard grey dropshadows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Solid `#0B0B0E`. Flat, non-reflective backdrop.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Resting):** Fill `#141419` with a subtle hairline stroke `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`. No shadow.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Card / Floating Navigation):** Fill `#1C1C24` backed by a gentle dark vignette shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`. Card scale transform: `scale(1.03)` with accelerated ease.
- **Level 3 (Modal / Video Overlay / Dropdown):** Solid `#141419` accompanied by `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and perimeter stroke `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.

### Crimson Glow Accents
- **Hero & Primary Action Glow:** Primary buttons and active badges incorporate a volumetric neon flare: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px rgba(255, 42, 58, 0.35)`.
- **Atmospheric Backlight:** Large decorative background radial gradients positioned at header corners: `radial-gradient(circle at 10% 20%, rgba(229, 9, 20, 0.15) 0%, transparent 60%)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces modern, fluid corners (`roundedness: 2`) that soften the sharp, dark cinematic look and match contemporary streaming app standards.

### Curvature Hierarchy
- **Standard UI Elements (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Small buttons, badge tags, input boxes, list items, and episode thumbnails.
- **Media Poster Cards (`1rem` / `16px`):** Media cards, carousel items, dialogs, and video preview containers (`rounded-xl`).
- **Surface Modules & Hero Banners (`1.5rem` / `24px`):** Hero cards, sidebar containers, and search overlay containers (`rounded-2xl`).
- **Fully Rounded / Pill (`9999px`):** Category filter chips, primary CTA buttons, floating status tags, search bars, and avatar rings.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Mulai Nonton", "Lanjutkan"):** Solid `#FF2A3A` fill, white text, bold font, pill-rounded (`rounded-full` or `rounded-xl`), accompanied by a soft crimson shadow (`rgba(255, 42, 58, 0.35)`). Includes a crisp leading playback icon.
- **Secondary CTA ("Daftar Sekarang", "Watchlist"):** Translucent dark fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` or `#1C1C24`), white text, 1px perimeter border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`). On hover, subtle background illumination (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.14)`).
- **Destructive/Ghost Actions ("Hapus"):** Dark translucent red outline (`border: 1px solid rgba(255, 42, 58, 0.4)`), text color `#FF2A3A`, transparent background.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Active State:** Solid crimson red (`#FF2A3A` or `#E50914`), white bold text, rounded pill shape.
- **Inactive State:** Low-profile background (`#1C1C24`), muted white/slate text (`#94A3B8`), subtle border. Hover transitions smoothly to lighter slate (`#262632`).

### Poster & Video Cards
- **Poster Thumbnail:** 2:3 vertical aspect ratio, `rounded-xl` (16px), overflow-hidden, subtle stroke. Contains top-right resolution badge ("HD"), and bottom gradient scrim revealing title, release info, and tertiary star rating (`#FFAA00` star + `#FFFFFF` score).
- **Episode List Card:** Horizontal row containing a 16:9 thumbnail, episode number, duration, title, and playback indicator. Clean divider border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` between episodes.

### Navigation Bar & Search Input
- **Top Navigation Bar:** Fixed or sticky top bar, translucent obsidian (`rgba(11, 11, 14, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`). Contains branding logo with crimson signature, category links (Beranda, Drama, Film, Anime, Katalog), and trailing notification bell + avatar ring.
- **Search Input:** Capsule or `rounded-xl` enclosure filled with `#141419`, 1px faint border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`), left-aligned magnifying glass icon, placeholder text in `#94A3B8`. Focus state shifts border to vibrant crimson (`#FF2A3A`).

### Badges & Star Ratings
- **Score Chip:** Dark background `#1C1C24` with golden star (`#FFAA00`) and white text (`8.5`, `9.5`).
- **Tag Badges (e.g., "Drama", "Thriller"):** Minimalist dark pill badges (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`) with muted white text (`#E2E8F0`), padding `4px 10px`.
